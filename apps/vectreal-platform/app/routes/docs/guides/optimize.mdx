# Optimizing & Configuring

The Publisher sidebar gives you full control over how your model looks and performs. This guide explains every setting.

---

## Quality presets

Quality presets apply a combination of Draco geometry compression, duplicate removal, and texture compression to reduce file size. Choose the preset that matches your use case:

| Preset | Texture resolution | Texture quality | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Original** | Unchanged | Unchanged | You want the model exactly as uploaded |
| **Maximum quality** | 2K WebP | 90% | High-fidelity previews and final renders |
| **Balanced** | 1K WebP | 80% | Recommended for most web experiences |
| **Smallest** | 512px WebP | 70% | Smallest file size, mobile and low-end hardware |

The three optimizing presets share the same geometry pipeline: duplicate removal and Draco compression, both on. They differ only in texture resolution and quality. **Original** runs nothing, Draco included, so the published file is your upload as it is. Mesh simplification and vertex quantization are off in every preset - simplification is destructive and opt-in from the advanced panel, and quantization is redundant because Draco quantizes vertex attributes itself.

Every preset is made from your original upload, never from the result of the previous one, so you can switch between them freely and pick **Original** to get the upload back. Choosing a preset applies it straight away; edits in the advanced panel apply when you press **Apply changes**. Saving stores the result of the preset in effect.

A scene that was saved optimized does not keep its original upload, so when you reopen it, presets start from the saved version and the first choice reads **Saved version**.

---

## Mesh simplification

Mesh simplification is off in every preset and is opt-in from the advanced settings panel, because it changes topology and can leave holes and shading seams.

Under the hood it uses the [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev) `simplify` function. The **ratio** is the fraction of vertices retained (1.0 = no simplification, 0.5 = keep half), offered as Light (keep 75%), Moderate (keep 50%), and Aggressive (keep 25%). A separate deviation limit (Strict, Balanced, Relaxed) stops the simplifier early, so the actual reduction is often smaller than the target.

## Texture compression

Textures are re-encoded in the browser using `OffscreenCanvas` (hardware-accelerated where available). The target format is WebP by default (JPEG and PNG are also supported), and the **quality** value is an encoder quality percentage in the `0`-`100` range (the presets use 90, 80, and 70).

> Texture compression runs in the browser with no server round-trip. The viewport preview uses the uncompressed original while compression runs.

## Lighting & environment

### Environment preset

Choose from a set of HDR environment presets that determine ambient lighting, reflections, and image-based lighting (IBL). Presets are grouped into four categories: **Studio** (`studio-key`, `studio-natural`, `studio-soft`), **Nature** (`nature-moonlit`, `nature-park`, `nature-park-overcast`, `nature-snow`), **Night** (`night-building`, `night-city`, `night-pure-sky`, `night-stars`), and **Outdoor** (`outdoor-golden-hour`, `outdoor-noon`, `outdoor-overcast`, `outdoor-sky`).

### Background

Open **Background** in the Environment tool and turn on **Show** to render the HDR environment map behind the model, then tune **Blur** and **Brightness**. With the toggle off the background stays transparent. There is no solid background color setting; set the page background behind the embed instead.

## Camera

### Initial view

There are no x/y/z inputs. Orbit the viewport to the framing you want, then click **Set camera to current view** to capture it onto the selected camera. The stored position, rotation, target, and FoV are what the scene opens on.

### Field of view (FoV)

Adjust the perspective camera's FoV in degrees, either from the presets or the numeric input. Wider FoV (e.g. 90°) exaggerates depth; narrower (e.g. 30°) produces a more orthographic feel.

### Auto Rotate

Turn on **Auto rotate** in the Interaction tool to continuously orbit the model, and set **Rotation Speed** under **Speeds** to control how fast it turns. The speed is disabled while Auto rotate is off.

## Saving settings

Settings are saved with the scene when you click **Save**. They are restored every time you or a viewer loads the scene embed.
