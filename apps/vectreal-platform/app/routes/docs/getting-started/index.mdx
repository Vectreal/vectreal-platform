# Your First Model

Open a model in the Publisher, optimize it, and publish it to a page. It runs in your browser: there is nothing to install, and you need an account only when you save.

---

## What you'll need

- Nothing to start with. The Publisher has two sample models ready.
- Your own 3D file, when you want to use one: GLB, glTF + bin + textures, STL, FBX, or OBJ + mtl + textures
- A Vectreal account, to save, publish and embed. Trying, optimizing and downloading need none.

---

## Step 1 - Open the Publisher with a sample

Go to [/publisher](/publisher). It opens on an empty stage that asks for a file. Below that, under **Or open a sample**, pick one:

- **Rocket** is mostly geometry, so it shows what geometry compression does.
- **Camera** carries nine 4K textures, so it shows what texture compression does.

The tile shows how much of the model has downloaded, then the model opens in the viewport. To go straight there, [open the rocket](/publisher?sample=rocket).

---

## Step 2 - Or upload your own model

Drag your 3D file anywhere onto the stage, or click **Choose a file** in the middle of it.

**glTF bundles**: a `.gltf` comes with a `.bin` and its textures. Drag the whole folder onto the stage, or use **Choose a folder** to pick it.

**Supported formats**

| Format | Extension | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| glTF | `.gltf` + `.bin` + textures | Preferred format for full fidelity |
| GLB | `.glb` | Self-contained binary glTF |
| STL | `.stl` | Geometry only - the format carries no materials or textures |
| FBX | `.fbx` | Textures usually travel inside the file |
| OBJ | `.obj` + `.mtl` + textures | A bundle, like glTF - upload the folder |

STL, FBX and OBJ are converted to glTF on the way in, so what you optimize and publish is a glTF either way. See [Upload](/docs/guides/upload) for per-format notes.

---

## Step 3 - Optimize

The card at the bottom right of the stage shows the scene's size and how long it takes to load on 4G. Click that line to open **Optimize Scene** on the left.

- Pick **Maximum quality**, **Balanced** or **Smallest**, then **Apply Optimizations**.
- Mesh simplification stays off unless you turn it on under **Advanced controls**.

The size on the card updates when the pass finishes. Close the panel with the **X** in its corner.

---

## Step 4 - Set up the scene

The toolbar on the left opens one panel each for **Environment**, **Shadows**, **Camera**, **Interaction** and **Hotspots**. Changes apply to the viewport as you make them.

Want the file rather than a published scene? Open the publish panel (Step 6) and use **Download** to export GLB, glTF, Draco-compressed GLB or USDZ. No account needed.

---

## Step 5 - Save the scene

- If you're signed in, click **Save** in the header and the scene is saved to your account. The location picker beside the scene name chooses its project and folder first.
- If you're not, the same button reads **Sign In to Save**. It takes you to sign in or create an account, and the scene is kept in this browser and comes back when you return.

Saved scenes appear in your [Dashboard](/dashboard).

---

## Step 6 - Publish the scene

Click the card at the bottom right of the stage to open **Scene Info & Publish**. Once the scene has been saved, the panel has a **Publish** section: publish from there and confirm.

Publishing saves your latest changes first, moves the scene from **draft** to **published**, and gives it a stable embed URL.

---

## Step 7 - Embed on a page

The **Embed** section of the same panel has the snippet. It looks like:

```html
<style>
  /* Lets the embed fill a bare flex parent, such as a Shopify Custom Liquid block. */
  @layer { div:not([class]):not([style]):has(> .vctrl-embed) { flex-grow: 1; } }
</style>
<div class="vctrl-embed" style="width: 100%; max-width: 100%; height: 400px;">
  <iframe
    src="https://vectreal.com/embed/<projectId>/<sceneId>?token=vctrl_your_key_here"
    style="width: 100%; height: 100%; border: 0;"
    allow="autoplay; xr-spatial-tracking"
    allowfullscreen
  ></iframe>
</div>
```

Paste all of it into any HTML page, the `<style>` block included. The embedded viewer loads your published model. [Publishing & Embedding](/docs/guides/publish-embed#why-the-snippet-ships-a-stylesheet) explains the stylesheet.

> A **preview API key** is required for external iframe access, and the embedding site must be on the **owning project's** allowed domain list - the list belongs to the project, not to the key, so every key scoped to that project shares it. A project with an empty list rejects every external host. Create a key in [Dashboard → API Keys](/dashboard/api-keys) and set the domains in [Dashboard → Projects](/dashboard/projects).
